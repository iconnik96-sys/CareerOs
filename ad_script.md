---

### 2. Google Flow (Veo) "Ingredients" Strategy for Consistency
To make sure the character in Shot 1 and Shot 2 looks like the same person and shares the same cinematic look, configure Google Flow's ingredient inputs as follows:

1. **Character Consistency Reference (Subject Ingredient):**
   - Upload 1 clear reference portrait of your chosen actor/subject (neutral lighting, headshot).
   - In Google Flow, tag this uploaded asset as the primary **Subject Ingredient** in both Shot 1 and Shot 2 prompts.
2. **Style Reference (Look & Feel Ingredient):**
   - Upload 1 reference frame featuring modern tech commercial lighting (cool cyan/indigo shadows with warm key lights).
   - Tag this as the **Style Reference** for both prompts to ensure identical film grain, lens depth, and color grading.
3. **Negative Guidance in Flow / Veo:**
   - Always append `"no text on screen, no subtitles, no watermark, no cartoonish artifacts"` in the negative prompt field or at the prompt's conclusion.

## 🛠️ Step-by-Step CapCut / Canva Assembly Instructions

1. **Import Veo Generations:** Download both 4-second 9:16 clips from Google Flow. Trim Shot 1 to `0:00 – 0:04` and Shot 2 to `0:04 – 0:08`.
2. **Add 1-Second End Card:** Append your 1.0s static graphic at `0:08 – 0:09` with a quick 0.1s crossfade transition.
3. **Synchronize Voiceover:** Place the VO track so *"Stop getting ghosted"* finishes right before Shot 2 begins, and *"Turn your resume into job offers with CareerOS"* plays seamlessly across Shot 2.
4. **Generate Auto-Captions:** In CapCut, enable dynamic word-by-word captions (preset style: "Bounce" or "Glow", centered at bottom third).
5. **Add Sound Effects:**
   - `0:00.0` - Tension riser
   - `0:02.8` - UI notification chime
   - `0:04.0` - Beat drop + swoosh
   - `0:08.0` - Success bell / chime
6. **Export Settings:** 1080 x 1920, 30 FPS, Bitrate: High (H.264 / MP4).
